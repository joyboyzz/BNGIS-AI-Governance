# 🎬 BNGIS — Demo Video Script (6 minutes)

> **How to use this script:** Run the app (`bash run.sh` → http://localhost:8000), set your screen recorder to 1080p (OBS Studio is free), keep this script on a second device, and read the narration naturally — don't memorize word-by-word. Timestamps tell you how long each section should take. Total: **~6 minutes**.

---

## ⏱️ 0:00 – 0:35 | THE HOOK (Dashboard page)

**SCREEN:** Dashboard page, slow scroll down through KPI cards and charts.

**SAY:**
> "Every year, India loses **₹3.8 lakh crore** to undelivered schemes — and 67% of eligible citizens don't even know what they're entitled to. The problem isn't a shortage of schemes. It's the missing *intelligence* that connects citizens to them.
>
> I'm Praveen Sudireddy, and this is **BNGIS — the Bharath Neuro-Governance Intelligence System** — an AI brain that connects every citizen's need to every government resource, in real time. Let me show you the live system."

---

## ⏱️ 0:35 – 1:45 | SCHEME MATCHING ENGINE (Module 1 + 3)

**SCREEN:** Scheme Matching page. Click the **"Lakshmi, 34"** persona button. Then click **⚡ Run AI Match**. Let the results load, scroll slowly.

**SAY:**
> "This is the Scheme Matching Engine. Instead of a citizen searching hundreds of scheme websites, BNGIS builds a **neural profile** — a 10-dimensional need vector covering income, location, occupation, social category and vulnerability.
>
> I'll pick a demo citizen — *Lakshmi, 34, a rural daily-wage worker from Karnataka* — and run the AI match.
>
> In under 100 milliseconds, the engine checked her against **21 real government schemes** — hard eligibility rules, cosine similarity, and a priority score that weighs benefit, success probability, effort and urgency.
>
> She qualifies for **9 schemes**, and the engine built her an **optimal portfolio of 4**: five lakh rupees of health cover under Ayushman Bharat, a pucca house under PMAY-G, ₹24,000 a year from Gruha Lakshmi, and free bus travel under Shakti. That's roughly **₹44,000 of value per year** she was invisible to.
>
> And see the 'nearest misses' section — the AI explains *why* the other schemes were blocked. Fully explainable, no black box. And every match is recorded on the transparency blockchain — we'll come back to that."

---

## ⏱️ 1:45 – 2:40 | RESOURCE OPTIMIZATION CORTEX (Module 2)

**SCREEN:** Resource Finder. Area = **Vijayanagar**, Type = **Hospitals**. Point at the orange reroute alert, then the network map.

**SAY:**
> "Next — public resources. India has 34% of hospital beds sitting empty while the *nearest* hospital turns patients away. BNGIS solves this with the Resource Optimization Cortex.
>
> I'm in Vijayanagar, Mysuru. The nearest hospital is only 0.7 kilometres away — but it's running at **91% capacity**. So the AI computed an allocation score for every facility — distance, live availability, quality — and issued a **reroute advisory**: a slightly farther hospital with 32 free beds and 40 minutes less waiting.
>
> The live network map shows every hospital, colour-coded by load — green is healthy, red is overloaded — with the AI's top picks connected by routing lines. The system always knows where the empty beds are."

---

## ⏱️ 2:40 – 3:30 | DISASTER RESPONSE (Module 5)

**SCREEN:** Disaster Response page. Point at SEIR chart, then scenario table, then flood table.

**SAY:**
> "BNGIS doesn't just react — it predicts. This is a live **SEIR epidemic simulation** — the same compartmental model epidemiologists use — running for a dengue outbreak in a population of one million.
>
> With no action, the peak is over **162,000 concurrent cases**. But watch the scenario table: strong containment cuts it to 48,000, and a full AI-guided response collapses it by **99%**.
>
> The peak is then cross-checked against the real hospital network from the previous module — and here it flags a **bed deficit of 3,496**, automatically recommending field hospitals and inter-district rerouting.
>
> Below that — monsoon flood risk for 12 Karnataka districts, scored on rainfall, river levels, terrain and history. Kodagu is SEVERE at 91.6 — and for each high-risk district the AI has already generated a response plan: 93 shelters, water tankers, medical teams, boats, and early-warning SMS in local languages."

## ⏱️ 3:30 – 4:20 | CITIZEN VOICE AI (Module 8)

**SCREEN:** Citizen Voice page. Click the **Hindi bribe sample**, then **🧠 Analyze Grievance**. Point at each result box.

**SAY:**
> "Now, the citizen's voice. A farmer who can't type English can still be heard. I'll click this Hindi grievance — *"for the ration card, the middleman is demanding 500 rupees."*
>
> The AI detected the language — Hindi — scored the sentiment, and classified the intent. And here's the important part: the word 'dalal' — middleman — is weighted four times higher than ordinary category words, so this complaint escalates straight to the **Lokayukta anti-corruption bureau**, not the ration office where the bribe taker sits.
>
> It generates a priority ticket, an SLA, and a recommended action — and records it on the blockchain. The system works in **English, Hindi, Telugu and Kannada**, fully offline, zero paid APIs."

## ⏱️ 4:20 – 5:20 | CORRUPTION SHIELD + BLOCKCHAIN ATTACK (Module 4 + 7)

**SCREEN:** Corruption Shield → **Run Full Audit**. Scroll through gauges and flagged table. Then Transparency Chain → **Simulate Hacker Attack** → show the red tamper alert → **Repair**.

**SAY:**
> "Now the Corruption Detection Shield — five AI layers auditing over 1,250 government transactions.
>
> Layer one, **Benford's Law** — manipulated ledgers break the natural distribution of first digits. Layers two to four: statistical outliers, vendor concentration, and temporal patterns like payments just below approval thresholds. Layer five: ghost beneficiaries caught by fuzzy name matching.
>
> In this seeded dataset, I injected four real fraud patterns — and the AI caught every one: threshold-splitting in Public Works, weekend round-amount payments in Education, a fiscal-year-end spike in the Water Department — while the clean departments stayed correctly LOW.
>
> Finally — can we trust the AI itself? Let me simulate a hacker editing a signed record... verification recomputes every SHA-256 hash... **caught**. The tamper is detected instantly, and one click restores the authentic ledger. That's governance you can audit."

## 🎥 Recording checklist

- [ ] App running at localhost:8000, browser zoom ~110%, dark room theme
- [ ] Run one full practice pass before recording
- [ ] Refresh pages just before showing them (fresh spinners feel "live")
- [ ] Mouse slow & deliberate — point at what you mention
- [ ] Record in 1080p, export as MP4
- [ ] Optional: soft background music at −25 dB (remove if unsure)
- [ ] Re-record a section if you fumble — don't restart the whole video
- [ ] For the viva: keep the Corruption + Blockchain demo for last — the hacker-attack catch is your mic-drop moment 🎤
